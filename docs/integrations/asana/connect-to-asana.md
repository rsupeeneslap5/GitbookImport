# Connect to Asana

SlapFive connects securely to Asana using OAuth authentication. No API keys or developer configuration are required. You or your Asana Admin can simply connect right from within SlapFive. You only have to do this once and it will be used for all data synchronization scenarios. The login data is then encrypted and stored within the Embedded Integration.

### Before you begin

* You must have an active Asana account.
* Your Asana account must have access to the workspace, projects, and tasks you want to integrate with SlapFive.
* If your organization restricts third-party applications, your Asana administrator may need to approve the connection.
* We recommend using an account that will maintain access to the relevant Asana resources over time rather than an individual user's account.

### How to connect

1. In SlapFive, go into **Settings**, click on the **Integrations** sub-tab, and click the **Asana Connection** box to expand it.
2. Click **Connect** and the Asana login screen appears. Sign in to your Asana account.
3. Review and authorize the requested permissions.

Once you are successfully connected, the button should now say **Disconnect**, and you should see a green <mark style="color:green;">**Connection success**</mark> message.
