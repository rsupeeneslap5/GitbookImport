# Activities

Records of specific actions performed by a Member on a given date. Each Activity is associated with an Activity Type and contributes to engagement tracking.

<img src="../../.gitbook/assets/unknown (19).png" alt="" height="276" width="436">

### Activity Score (Engagement Score)

A point value assigned to each Activity Type. A Member’s total Engagement Score is the sum of all Activity Scores from the Activities they have completed.
