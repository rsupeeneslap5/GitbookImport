# Prompts and Surveys

**Start With the Outcome**

Every survey should answer three simple questions:

* What do you want to learn?\
  Identify the customer feedback, profile information, content, or internal insight you need.
* How will you use it?\
  Connect what you collect to a larger program or business objective.
* What happens next?\
  Determine how responses will be activated, whether that means creating content, updating customer data, triggering follow-up, identifying advocates, or analyzing trends. SlapFive Surveys

\
**Use Cases**

**Measure Customer Sentiment**\
Run NPS, onboarding, or other feedback surveys while keeping responses connected to the customer record for follow-up and future engagement. SlapFive Surveys

**Capture Customer Voice & Content**\
Collect customer experiences, outcomes, challenges, testimonials, and feedback through text, audio, or video, then turn those responses into reusable Stories. SlapFive Surveys

**Build Content & Summary Boards**\
Capture responses at scale and organize the resulting customer voice, themes, proof points, and Stories into Boards that teams can easily use.

**Create Win-Wires**\
Survey account teams after a win to capture what differentiated your company, what influenced the decision, and what other sellers can learn. Turn those responses into internal Stories and sales enablement content. SlapFive Surveys

**Re-engage Advocates**\
Reconnect with existing advocates to understand their current interests, preferences, and willingness to participate in advocacy activities.

**Onboard Advocates & Keep Profiles Current**\
Capture interests, advocacy preferences, profile information, gifting restrictions, and other attributes, then use responses to keep customer profiles up to date. SlapFive Surveys

**Capture Referrals**\
Give customers a simple way to recommend peers and provide the context your team needs to follow up.

**Capture Content for AEO & Human Audiences**\
Collect authentic customer perspectives, experiences, outcomes, and language at scale to support customer stories, proof points, FAQs, and AEO initiatives.

**Collect Customer Award Submissions**\
Create a structured way to capture award nominations, customer outcomes, supporting details, and reusable customer stories.

**Nominate New Advocates**\
Give internal teams a simple way to nominate customers for your advocacy program and capture the context needed for onboarding and follow-up.



### From Response to Action

The value of a survey isn't the response itself. It's what you can do with it.

Survey responses can become:

* Customer data that enriches profiles and preferences
* Customer Stories for marketing, sales, and advocacy
* Boards that make customer insights easy to discover and share
* Advocacy signals that identify the right customers for the right opportunities
* Workflows that automatically trigger the next step
*   AI-powered insights that surface themes and supporting customer voice at scale SlapFive Surveys





#### The result: one scalable way to listen to your customers, understand what they’re telling you, and turn that input into action.
