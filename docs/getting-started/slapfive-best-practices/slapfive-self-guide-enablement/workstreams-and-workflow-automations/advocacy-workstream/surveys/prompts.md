# Prompts

Types of Prompts

* **Direct Prompts:** Sent straight to registered SlapFive Members through the platform or automated workflows.
* **Open Prompts:** Public-facing links you can paste into emails, web pages, or community posts where anyone (even non-members) can respond by entering their name, company, and email.
* **Prompt Groups (Surveys):** A collection of multiple prompts grouped together into a single questionnaire.
* **Evergreen vs. One-Time Prompts:** Settings that control whether a prompt stays active indefinitely or disappears for a user after they have completed it once.

Response FormatsSlapFive lets customers reply using whichever format works best for them:

* **Video** (recorded via smartphone or webcam)
* **Audio** recordings
* **Text** written responses
* **Numeric** ratings or scores
* **File or Link** uploads

What Happens After a Prompt?

* **Stories:** Customer responses are turned into structured content assets called **Stories**.
* **AI Writing:** You can use SlapFive's AI tools (or _CaseStudyBot.ai_) to turn prompt responses into case studies, blog posts, or social media updates.
* **Boards:** Curated landing pages where you publish and showcase one or more customer Stories to sales teams or the public.

If you are looking to build a specific type of prompt or campaign in SlapFive, let me know your **goal** (e.g., video testimonials, sales win-wires, or peer reviews) and I can help outline the right questions to ask.
