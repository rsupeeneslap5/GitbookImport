# Workshops during Implementation

### Data Model Workshop:

This workshop runs in parallel with kickoff and early discovery. Together, we clarify where your data lives, what will be migrated, and what resources are needed.

Workshop Focus:

* Understand what data and content you have today
* Decide what should be imported into SlapFive
* Define any dynamic custom fields
* Review data formatting for a smooth upload (from spreadsheets or legacy systems)

Preparation:\
Guided by your completed Data Decisions Questionnaire, required before the session.

Attendees:\
Champion, SlapFive Customer Success, optional SOPs/MOPs



### Workstream Workshops:

What to Expect: Roll up your sleeves - we’ll define the automations, workflows, and system integrations needed to launch your first Workstream in 30 days.

Workshop Types (as needed):

* CMA System of Record: Nominations, Advocate Sourcing, SSOT workflows, etc.
* Advanced Reference Automation: Content Suggestions, Recorded References, Requesting & Fulfilling workflows, etc.
* Customer Referrals: One to one and one to many workflows
* AI-Powered Customer Proof: Customer content workflows

These sessions ensure your Phase 1 Workstream is fully designed, automated, and ready for Go-Live.
